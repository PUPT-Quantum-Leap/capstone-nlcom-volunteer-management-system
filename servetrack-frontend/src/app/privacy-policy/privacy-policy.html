Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

New Life Community Care Foundation International, Inc. (NLCOM) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the ServeTrack volunteer management platform.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. This includes:

👤
Personal Information

Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address

🔒
Account Information

Username, password, and security questions

📋
Volunteer Profile

Skills, interests, availability, and emergency contact information

📊
Activity Data

Volunteer hours, events attended, and contributions made

💬
Communication Data

Messages between volunteers and organizers, notifications preferences

🖱️
Usage Data

Information about how you use and interact with the platform

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our volunteer management services
  • Process your registration and manage your volunteer account
  • Connect you with relevant volunteer opportunities and events
  • Track and verify your volunteer hours and activities
  • Send you updates, notifications, and communications about volunteer opportunities
  • Generate reports and analytics for volunteer program management
  • Protect the security and integrity of our platform

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • With Volunteer Organizers: Share necessary information with event organizers so they can coordinate volunteer activities
  • Service Providers: With third-party vendors who perform services on our behalf (e.g., hosting, data analysis)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities
  • Protection of Rights: To protect the rights, property, or safety of NLCOM, our volunteers, or others
  • Business Transfers: In connection with any merger, sale of company assets, or acquisition

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Encryption Security Audits Access Controls Secure Storage Staff Training

6. Your Rights and Choices

Access Request a copy of your data
Correction Request correction of errors
Deletion Request deletion of data
Opt-out Opt-out of communications
Data Portability Request data in standard format

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided at the end of this policy.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

For volunteer registration, we require volunteers to be at least 16 years old. Parents or guardians of volunteers between 16-18 years old should supervise their children's use of the platform.

8. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of every site you visit.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this privacy policy periodically for any changes.
