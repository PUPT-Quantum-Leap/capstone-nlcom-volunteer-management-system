About NLCOM

New Life Community Care Foundation International, Inc. (NLCOM) was established to RESPOND to the immediate needs of a community after a disaster or calamity, REACH OUT to help in disaster preparedness and community outreach programs, REBUILD lives of families and communities as they recover after a crisis or disaster.

NLCOM is the compassion arm of New Life and is committed to organizing activities from disaster preparedness trainings, relief goods distribution, mobile kitchen feeding operations, Psychological First Aid to kids and adults traumatized by a disaster, and rebuilding of damaged houses.

Our Vision

Demonstrating love and compassion by responding to needs, reaching out, and rebuilding lives.

Our Mission

Provide natural and spiritual needs of disaster-stricken communities by partnering with local churches, non-government organizations, private institutions, individuals and different government agencies.
