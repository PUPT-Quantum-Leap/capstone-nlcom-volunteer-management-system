================================================================================
SERVETRACK CODEBASE - COMPREHENSIVE ANALYSIS
Volunteer Management System: Angular 21 + Laravel 12
================================================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
=================
ServeTrack is a production-ready monorepo with Angular 21 frontend and Laravel 12
backend. It implements volunteer management with RSVPs, availability tracking,
incident command system, and admin dashboard. Fully dockerized with automated
CI/CD, comprehensive testing, and security scanning.
